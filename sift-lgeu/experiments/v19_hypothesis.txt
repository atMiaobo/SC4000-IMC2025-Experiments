of 0.75: a candidate match (m, n) is admitted only if m.distance < 0.75 * n.distance. For video
sequences with repetitive staircase textures and small inter-frame overlap, many true
correspondences fall in the 0.75-0.80 range — the nearest descriptor is a correct match, but the
second-nearest is nearly as close because staircase step edges repeat across the image at multiple
scales. This causes valid correspondences to be rejected, artificially depressing Lowe match counts
and preventing outlier images from accumulating the 30+ matches needed to form any valid edge. V19

MAX_EXHAUSTIVE_N, >=70% timestamp filenames). MAGSAC geometric verification (MIN_MAGSAC_INLIERS=3)

with many incidental Lowe matches at ratio=0.80 will still fail the geometric consistency check. V19
also incorporates V18's relaxed COLMAP mapper thresholds for video scenes (init_min_num_inliers 5→3,
init_min_tri_angle 1.0→0.5 degrees, abs_pose_min_num_inliers 4→3), which was previously

stairs images accumulate enough matches to form a valid SIFT edge and enter COLMAP reconstruction,
