V26 Hypothesis: Combine V25's merge-all-sub-reconstructions with V24's init_min_num_inliers=3 for video scenes. All prior versions V7–V25 use init_min_num_inliers=5 for video, meaning COLMAP requires at least 5 verified F-matrix inliers before accepting a seed pair for initialization. For staircase video with nearly-parallel optical axes, the geometrically best available seed pair often produces only 3–5 F-matrix inliers, causing COLMAP to reject it and either fall back to a geometrically worse alternative or fail initialization entirely. This explains why scene_0 (15 images) and scene_2 (20 images) plateau at 5 and 3 registered cameras respectively despite V21's abs_pose_min_num_inliers=3 lowering the per-frame localization threshold. V25 attempted to recover additional cameras by merging all COLMAP sub-reconstructions rather than taking only the largest, but tied V7 at 8/51 stairs cameras — indicating that COLMAP produces only one sub-reconstruction per scene under the current seed-pair selection rules, leaving the merge-all strategy nothing extra to collect. V24 tested init_min_num_inliers=3 independently but smoke_errored before the SfM step ran, leaving the combination with V25 untested. V26 lowers init_min_num_inliers from 5 to 3 (the P3P minimum) for video scenes: with a lower inlier floor, COLMAP accepts more seed pair candidates, increasing the probability of a successful initialization and potentially enabling the mapper to produce additional sub-reconstructions from image clusters that the floor-5 threshold currently rejects — sub-reconstructions that V26's merge-all strategy can then capture. init_min_tri_angle remains at 1.0 degrees (V22 demonstrated that 0.5 causes regression). Non-video and small datasets retain V7 settings unchanged, protecting ETs and hidden structured-capture datasets from regression.
