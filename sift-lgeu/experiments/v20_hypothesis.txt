V20 Hypothesis: Versions V14-V19 all tried to rescue the 14 outlier stairs images
by forcing SIFT edges at the Python level — through GBM num_gap neutralisation (V14),
lower Lowe-match thresholds (V15/V16), looser Lowe ratios (V19), and increased SIFT
feature density (V17). None succeeded: these 14 images cannot accumulate even 30 Lowe
matches with any of their top-20 GBM-selected neighbours under any tested parametrisation.
The SIFT edge gate is simply impassable for them. V20 abandons the SIFT-edge rescue
approach entirely and instead uses post-clustering outlier reassignment: after V16
clustering (GBM num_gap=0 + Lowe threshold=30) assigns images to scenes or "outliers",
each outlier is reassigned to the scene of its most visually similar non-outlier image
by cosine similarity of precomputed global descriptors. These outlier images are then
passed to COLMAP as members of their reassigned scene (scene_0 or scene_2). COLMAP
runs exhaustive matching on the enlarged scene using its own internal SIFT pipeline —
without the 30-match Python threshold — giving the outlier images a genuine second
chance at registration. If the outlier images share even sparse geometric overlap with
their reassigned scene neighbours, COLMAP's bundle adjustment can register them. Worst
case, they fail to register and receive identity poses, which is no worse than their
current status. SfM uses the proven V7 path (exhaustive for N<=100 video) to avoid
the upload failures seen in V18/V19. Non-video and small datasets (N<=40) are fully
unaffected and use standard V9 logic throughout.
