V15 Hypothesis: In V9 (GBM+TOPK=20), 14 of 51 stairs images are stranded as "outliers"
with 0 cameras registered. Stairs is a video sequence (N=51 > MAX_EXHAUSTIVE_N=40) with
forward-facing staircase motion; consecutive frames have limited visual overlap, so many
valid same-scene pairs produce only 30-39 Lowe-ratio matches — just below the standard
GOOD_MATCH_THRESHOLD of 40. These pairs would survive MAGSAC geometric verification
(inliers >= MIN_MAGSAC_INLIERS=3, confirming consistent camera geometry), but are blocked
from forming edges purely by the Lowe count cutoff, leaving their images with no valid
graph edges and thus stranded as outliers before COLMAP is even invoked. Previous fixes
(V12 temporal augmentation, V13 cosine-only ranking, V14 num_gap zeroing) all targeted
pair selection quality but left the threshold at 40, so the core cutoff problem remained.
V15 lowers good_match_threshold from 40 to 30 specifically for video sequences (N >
MAX_EXHAUSTIVE_N, >=70% timestamp filenames), where LGEU pair pre-selection (TOPK=20)
already limits the candidate set to the most visually similar pairs, making cross-scene
false positives unlikely, and MAGSAC (MIN_MAGSAC_INLIERS=3) provides a final geometric
guard. Non-video and small datasets use the unchanged V9 logic (threshold=40, TOPK=20).
We expect fewer outlier images for stairs (and any hidden video datasets), more registered
cameras overall, and no regression in ETs or non-video datasets.
