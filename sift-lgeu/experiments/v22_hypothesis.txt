V22 Hypothesis: V21 lowered abs_pose_min_num_inliers from 4 to 3 (the theoretical P3P
minimum) for video scenes and tied V7 at 8/51 stairs cameras registered. The remaining
untried COLMAP mapper parameter from V18 — which changed three parameters simultaneously
and upload_failed — is init_min_tri_angle. V21 preserves the V7 video default of 1.0
degrees for this parameter. For a forward-facing staircase recording, consecutive frames
share a narrow field of view and nearly-parallel optical axes; the true geometric baseline
between adjacent frames is small, so the triangulation angle of any candidate seed pair
often falls below 1.0 degrees. When COLMAP's incremental mapper evaluates seed pairs for
initialisation, it rejects those whose triangulation angle is below init_min_tri_angle,
forcing it to select a geometrically inferior seed pair — one with a wider apparent angle
but weaker photometric overlap. Starting from a poor seed pair produces a sparse initial
3D point cloud, which in turn limits how many subsequent frames can accumulate the three
inlier correspondences required for registration (even with abs_pose_min_num_inliers=3).
V22 lowers init_min_tri_angle from 1.0 to 0.5 degrees for video scenes only, allowing
COLMAP to select the geometrically best available seed pair. A better seed pair yields a
denser initial point cloud, which increases the likelihood that adjacent frames find three
or more inlier correspondences and can be registered. Combined with V21's
abs_pose_min_num_inliers=3, this targets the seed-initialisation bottleneck that V21 alone
could not address. Non-video scenes and small datasets (N <= MAX_EXHAUSTIVE_N) retain V7
settings unchanged, so ETs and hidden structured-capture datasets are unaffected.
