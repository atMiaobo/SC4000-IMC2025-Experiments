V25 Hypothesis: All prior versions V7–V24 call pycolmap.incremental_mapping() and then
take ONLY the largest sub-reconstruction with:
    best_rec = max(reconstructions.values(), key=lambda r: r.num_reg_images())
This silently discards every smaller sub-reconstruction that COLMAP produced. For the
stairs dataset, two physically separate camera rigs record simultaneously from different
positions. COLMAP's incremental mapper naturally produces multiple disjoint
sub-reconstructions — one per rig cluster — because the two rigs share no geometric
overlap and cannot be merged into a single model. The V9 diagnostics confirm this
structure: scene_0 (15 images, 3 cameras registered in V9) and scene_2 (20 images,
3 cameras) are two separate video scenes; V21 (the current best at 8/51) reaches 8
cameras by lowering abs_pose_min_num_inliers to 3. However, even within each scene,
COLMAP likely produces additional smaller sub-reconstructions (e.g., two isolated
temporal clusters within scene_0's 15 images that could not be bridged) that are
currently discarded. V25 merges poses from ALL sub-reconstructions returned by
incremental_mapping, sorting by descending registration count so that for any image
appearing in multiple sub-reconstructions, the pose from the largest (most globally
constrained) reconstruction wins. This approach does not change any matching or mapper
threshold — it simply recovers cameras that COLMAP correctly reconstructed but that
were being silently dropped. Scene clustering is unchanged from V21/V16 (GBM num_gap=0
+ Lowe threshold=30 for video, TOPK=20). Mapper settings are identical to V21
(abs_pose_min_num_inliers=3 for video, init_min_tri_angle=1.0, init_min_num_inliers=5).
Non-video and small datasets (N<=40) are fully unaffected — their exhaustive path
typically produces a single reconstruction, so merging all reconstructions is a no-op.
Expected outcome: if COLMAP produces 2+ sub-reconstructions per video scene (a plausible
scenario for dual-rig staircase recording), V25 could recover the cameras from secondary
sub-reconstructions, potentially exceeding V21's 8/51 stairs result.
